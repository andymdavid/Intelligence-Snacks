A business Dr Mitch worked with through Unveil disliked its HR platform. The layout was confusing. Its purple colour scheme felt aggressive, and the small text looked grey rather than black, making it hard for the owner to read.

The business used the subscription for employment contracts, recruitment interview templates and police checks. Mitch saw those tasks as basic automations and helped build a custom tool around them, using the business’s own branding and colours. The font and text size suited the owner. Text opacity was set to 100 per cent so the lettering would no longer look grey, and the buttons were big enough to see and click.

The business cancelled the subscription. According to Mitch, the custom replacement cost roughly the same, and the business was actually using it. The owner had an interface they could read and liked using.
