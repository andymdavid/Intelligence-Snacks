Jarrad built a harness of scripts to keep coding models to his standards. It worked for a while, but the work became slow and expensive. He stripped the harness out and says he got better results faster. In his view, the agents had become more capable and needed less hand-holding.

Now, when a codebase is set up the way he wants it, Jarrad worries less about agents going off on their own. He finds they tend to follow the path already there. Pete thinks the agents have also become better at exploring a codebase before making changes.

Jarrad draws a line at starting from scratch. Then he'd want to be involved in every decision. With the architecture in place, he's more comfortable letting agents work within it and spending more of his time deciding what the experience should be.
