Andy would like an accountant who's always on. Looking back three or six months leaves him with things he can't do much about now. Someone closer to the business could help diagnose a problem or work through tax strategy while he can still act on the advice. He wants help that's useful next week.

Getting that help can mean paying to have a question explored before you know what the research will turn up. Pete described the offer he imagines getting from a small accounting firm that promises to be more strategic. It could look into his question, but he'd be facing a speculative bill of five grand for the research.

That prospect puts him off asking, especially when he can start exploring the question with AI for a flat fee. He still has to guide it. An answer that enthusiastically agrees with his idea can seem wrong, and he pushes back when it doesn't make sense to him. Then he can use the reply to keep working through the question.
