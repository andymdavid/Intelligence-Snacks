Andy goes straight to code when exploring a product idea. He can open AI-generated HTML in a browser and see whether an interface makes sense and feels intuitive. Course corrections are quick. He keeps the parts he likes and drops the rest.

Jarrad often works with designs generated in chat, but uses Figma or Pen.dev when he needs to see the flows for a bigger feature. He talks through the output with an agent until he is satisfied enough to build. The design file is a temporary way to see the experience; he no longer needs an exhaustive record of every flow.

The work still calls for design judgement. Jarrad says models often put too much explanatory text in an interface, so he sets guardrails and refines what they produce. His Pen files may be messy, but he wants the design system in the product itself to be disciplined, using components and variables rather than hard-coded style values.
