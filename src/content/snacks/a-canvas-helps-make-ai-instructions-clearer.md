Andy has put every frontend component from Ambulando, an app he and Pete use, onto an inspectable canvas. He can click on a component, see how it works and ask an agent to change it. There's no need to move it into a separate design tool.

Take the corners of a component. Andy can point to the one he wants to adjust, ask the agent to trim its corners, then have it apply that treatment consistently across the stylesheet. He can show the agent where to start and explain how far the change should reach.

For Pete, the canvas helps him slow down. It gives him time to think through a solution and explore the details of what he wants, rather than trying to interpret everything through chat. He says taking that time to explain himself more clearly means fewer rounds of revision and results closer to what he imagined.
