Pete framed the objection to AI slop as an objection to carelessness rather than to AI itself. The problem begins when someone gives a system almost nothing, does nothing with what comes back and publishes it anyway. That work asks an audience to care about something its creator barely attended to.

His example was Gigi's approach to vibe coding apps. Gigi might begin with a 45-minute monologue explaining exactly how an app should come into the world, then let agents deconstruct those instructions and build it. He still inspects the result and iterates. The agent performs much of the construction, but it works from a detailed human conception and remains subject to human judgement.

Pete saw the same pattern in Intelligence Snacks. The podcast supplies recorded opinions and raw material, while AI works through that material and crafts possible expressions of it. The process resembles working with a designer who receives a substantial brief rather than inventing the substance unaided. The creator is present at the beginning and the end, first defining what matters and then deciding what is ready to publish.
