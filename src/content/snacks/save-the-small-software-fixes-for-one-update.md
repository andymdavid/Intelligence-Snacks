Pete was getting tired of always fixing the tools he was trying to use. While developing Ambulando, which he uses on his phone and Mac, he’d spot something wrong and push it into another update. Too many plates spinning. Sometimes a feature hadn’t carried over to another version; sometimes he just didn’t like how something worked.

None of it stopped him using the app. So he started collecting the annoyances in a daily thread and consolidating them into a list, rather than responding to each one as it came up.

When he’s heading out for an hour or two, he can hand that list to his AI agent and ask it to fix the problems and deploy across his devices. He comes back to one new update and one list of changes to run through.
