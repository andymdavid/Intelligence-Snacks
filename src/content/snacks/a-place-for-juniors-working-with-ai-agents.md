Pete has only so much attention to give the AI agents he works with. He can imagine someone alongside him, learning how he guides the agents and helping with the work. That could let his capacity grow sideways, through another person.

Pete suggests that a junior and senior might end up doing similar work at different levels. The junior could learn how to guide an agent while contributing to an actual task, with someone more experienced to turn to. He doesn’t claim to know exactly what the arrangement would look like.

Lincoln sees exposure and responsibility as ways to help juniors grow, especially when someone is there to support them through mistakes. He describes a former colleague, whom he thinks he mentored, who is developing ways to help younger workers practise critical thinking. For Lincoln, the question is how to connect that kind of expertise with juniors who need it.
