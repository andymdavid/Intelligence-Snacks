A truck is built around constraints, including the need for a driver. Take the driver out, and the best design may change too. Pete sees businesses the same way. Their size and structure depend partly on how much work a human can do, so changing that limit with AI gives you reason to rethink the organisation itself.

That means looking beyond faster tasks. In Pete’s view, businesses still have a purpose when agents take on more of the work, but they may need a different shape to do it well. A company that can’t adapt quickly enough could collapse. The question becomes how to organise the people and agents around the work that remains.

Human attention still has limits. Pete describes agents handling background jobs while people review the results and do work that still needs them, with the team sized to the attention the problem requires. He thinks companies may shrink without disappearing. Even with agents doing much of the execution, someone has to care enough to check that the result actually solves the problem.
