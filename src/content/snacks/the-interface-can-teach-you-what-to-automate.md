Andy built Snack Studio to run the podcast and produce its written content and website. He set up each pipeline carefully, testing it until he was confident it could produce an output he found acceptable. He didn't know what the finished process should be until he worked through it.

Once he understood the steps, he stripped the app back so an agent could work through them without him using the interface. Some results were a pleasant surprise. Andy thought the generated Snack thumbnails looked much better than the ones he had made himself.

Jarrad recognised a familiar product lifecycle in that work. Andy had to explore the process, feel the pain points and iterate before handing it over. The interface gave him a way to learn what the workflow needed before he reduced his own interaction with it.
