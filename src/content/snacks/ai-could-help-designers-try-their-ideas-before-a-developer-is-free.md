A hardware worker could wait six months for software help before getting to try an idea. Pete suggests AI could let them have a go while they're waiting, learning some of the software team's job along the way. A designer waiting for a developer could do much the same, using AI to attempt the code for a design they can't yet implement themselves.

In that version of the work, the designer might get roughly 80% of the implementation done before handing it to a developer to finish competently. By then, they've had to try making their design work. They can bring the developer an unfinished implementation and a better understanding of what's involved in building it.

Trying to put that work to use could also reveal why the idea won't work as planned. A legal constraint might prevent it, or the people expected to buy it might turn out to want something quite different. The designer would have to rethink what they're making before asking the developer to carry on with it.
