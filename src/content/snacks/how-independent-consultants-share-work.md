When a client asks Lincoln for help beyond his own expertise, he can bring in another consultant while contributing what he already knows about the client's business and culture. But finding the right person depends on whom he knows and whether they are available when the work comes in.

Lincoln is building Leen to connect independent consultants around work. Sharing an opportunity can be a hard decision for a solo consultant who needs the income, he says, but he has found that people he brings in also bring him into their projects. The relationship gives him colleagues to exchange ideas with as well as a way to share work.

He wants the network to meet in person as well as online, with local breakfasts and sundowners where consultants can get to know each other. More than 260 people had registered their interest at the time of the conversation. As Pete observes, handing work to someone else is easier when trust has had a chance to develop.
