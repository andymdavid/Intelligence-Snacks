When Andy has too many open tasks to review, he asks Lara, his AI agent, to triage them. She checks why tasks are still open and whether anything has been forgotten, then points him to the messages that need his attention. The task comments can be complicated working notes. Without that extra pass, he struggles to know where to pick up the work.

But the review itself needs trust. Andy worries that swarms of agents could multiply the work without expanding his ability to supervise it. Handing the checking to another agent helps with the volume, though he still relies on that agent to judge whether the work is correct.

Pete compares this to running a large business, where you can’t supervise everyone yourself. He suggests a layer of managers and says their incentives would need to align with the work they oversee. He expects human teams to shrink rather than disappear. With agents handling background coordination, people would still need to review the work, and the team would need enough people to give the problem the attention it requires.
