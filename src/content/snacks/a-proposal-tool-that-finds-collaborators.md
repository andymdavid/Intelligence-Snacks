Lincoln says Leen's proposal generator draws on a consultant's case studies, experience and capabilities to help create a pitch. The tool also helps consultants build their case studies.

As a consultant builds a proposal, the generator can flag a gap in capacity or capability and suggest people in the network to add. That brings potential collaborators into the conversation while the work is still being pitched.

Lincoln expects clients to ask where a consultant has done similar work before, even if a proposal sets out a convincing approach. Leen's verification process lets consultants send a case study to the client they worked with for confirmation. The client can choose to anonymise it.
