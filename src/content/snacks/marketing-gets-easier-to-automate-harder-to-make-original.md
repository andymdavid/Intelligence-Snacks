A marketing team can set up AI pipelines to publish written content and thousands of videos across channels. Andy's concern is that everyone else can do that too. If customers stop paying attention to feeds full of the same material, producing more won't necessarily help a business reach them.

The team still needs to find a customer. Automating the ads or videos reduces the work of making them, but leaves unanswered the harder question of how they will get anyone's attention. A pipeline can do exactly what the team asked and still leave it struggling to break through.

That's why Andy expects marketing to remain difficult as production gets easier. He thinks familiar methods will become commonplace faster, pushing businesses to think more originally about the problems they're trying to solve. Understanding a customer's pain and finding a distinctive way to address it takes work that publishing another batch of videos doesn't answer.
