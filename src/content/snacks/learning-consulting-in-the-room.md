Early in his career at Accenture, Lincoln was asked to help develop a quality assurance operating model for a contact centre. He spoke with colleagues in Canada and London, who shared their knowledge and frameworks. Then he worked with a managing director to shape the approach and joined her at a client meeting.

Lincoln watched how she managed the room. Afterwards, she asked what he'd taken from the experience. He considers that kind of learning hard to replace, even as AI makes knowledge easier to access.

Now Lincoln is working with universities on programmes that would connect graduates and students with consultants. He wants them to develop critical thinking and communication by learning alongside experienced practitioners. He also sees an opportunity to work with solo independents and see how they win and deliver work while running their own businesses.
