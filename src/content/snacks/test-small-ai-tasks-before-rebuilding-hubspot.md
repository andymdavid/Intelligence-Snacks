Spending six months rebuilding HubSpot is too long to wait for a result, says Dr Mitch, who helps small businesses build their operations at Unveil. He starts by mapping where AI might save money or reduce administration, then recommends testing in small increments. A business that hates its customer relationship management software can start with a smaller task.

Mitch shows owners the follow-up material they've already received from him, including customised documents and a checklist sent after a call. He didn't assemble them manually in Word, Excel or Canva. His AI workflow uses the conversation alongside processes and resources he's already built to prepare material he can review and send. He's done the setup work. That lets him produce something specific to the client without filling in a generic template by hand.

Once owners see that, they ask whether they could do the same for their customers. For a trades business, Mitch suggests a quoting framework. He describes leaving a client meeting and dictating the price, the service, the roadmap and an upsell to offer into a proposal builder on his phone. The service agreement can go in too. That gives the business a small AI test to try on its next customer proposal.
