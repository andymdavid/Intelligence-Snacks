Long narrative reports are unnecessary for many aspects of ongoing AI agent work. A simpler records system can capture what was decided, which questions remain open, what still needs inspection and which information is worth preserving. This gives people and agents a reliable account of the project's progress without turning every handover into a fifty-page explanation.

The value lies in retrieval, not just storage. When someone asks about unresolved questions around one part of a project, an agent can find the relevant record and bring those questions into the discussion. Once answers emerge, the agent can update the record so the next person or agent has access to the latest information.

These records support human oversight and allow agents to pick up work where others left off. A person can inspect why a decision was made, while another agent can retrieve the context needed to continue. Detailed documents still have a place when precision and accountability matter, but routine progress doesn't need to become a comprehensive narrative. A useful handover can be as simple as an accessible record of decisions, outstanding questions and what needs attention next.
