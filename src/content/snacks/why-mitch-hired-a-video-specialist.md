Dr Mitch hired a short-form video specialist in the Philippines knowing he might automate most of the work. He runs SME advisory company Unveil. He says he's putting out at least ten pieces of content a day as part of his content strategy. By his estimate, he could build a Claude agent to do 80 to 90 per cent of the specialist's job. He wanted someone to talk to.

The specialist turned out to be a DJ. Mitch has a background in music, so they chat about DJing before discussing what to make for the month's video content and what ideas the specialist is seeing. That chance to swap ideas and have a laugh was part of why Mitch hired him.

Mitch says he enjoys discussing the work with another person enough to pay for it. He sees AI as giving business owners a choice about how to grow, including which jobs they want people to do. In this case, he chose the conversation as well as the videos.
