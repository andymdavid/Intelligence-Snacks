Losing access to Claude could stop work even without a data breach. Pete’s concern is control. He argues that owners need to keep their customer relationships and processes under their own control, so the business can keep operating when they remove a provider. Switching models should be possible without losing the way the business works.

Dr Mitch, who runs business consultancy Unveil, compares that dependence to keeping his books in Xero. He already accepts that risk. If Xero went down, he says he’d be scrambling to get across his accounts. Relying on AI across the rest of the business extends the same problem to quoting and customer follow-up, which could depend on a provider too.

Mitch takes that risk for speed. He describes dictating into his phone while shopping, asking Claude to prepare proposals and service agreements using processes and templates he has already built, ready for him to review. But he says those processes would still be there if the AI service disappeared. He could return to earlier ways of working, though he expects he would have to adapt them rather than simply restore the old setup.
