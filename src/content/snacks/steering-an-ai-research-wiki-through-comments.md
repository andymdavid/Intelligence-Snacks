Pete’s son was researching Derinkuyu, but useful books were hard to find. The local library could order a 30-page pamphlet. Pete set an AI agent to ask a new research question every hour and build a sourced wiki for the project.

His son could ask for changes right on the pages. When Pete checked the documents, he found comments asking for a different approach and spelling out what information his son needed. Those requests set off triggers and scheduled work, and new pages appeared in the wiki. He was steering the research from the documents he was reading.

Following the source links brought another difficulty. One led to a 600-page report in Turkish. Pete suggested using translation, or asking the agent to pull out relevant passages and quotations. He described the wiki as a gathering tool. His son could read the analysis, then go back to the source where possible to find material useful to his project.
