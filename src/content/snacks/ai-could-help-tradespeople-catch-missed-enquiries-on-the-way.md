A growing electrical business can leave its owner struggling to get back to everyone. Dr Mitch, who runs business advisory company Unveil, describes a voice-based AI workflow for checking missed enquiries between jobs. The owner could ask who had been in touch and who still needed a response, much as they would call the office dispatcher.

Mitch starts by asking where enquiries arrive and which ones go unanswered. His proposed workflow would check ServiceM8, Go High Level and Meta ads, along with Outlook and call history. Then it would match a missed enquiry to the map of scheduled jobs. Is that person on the way?

In his example, the AI could flag a prospect between the current job and the next one. The tradesperson could stop and quote within fifteen minutes, with a chance of winning the work. They would already be passing through.
