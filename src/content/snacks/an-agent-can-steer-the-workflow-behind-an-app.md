Snack Studio was the app Andy wanted. It turned podcast material into articles for the Intelligence Snacks website, with a pleasant place to edit the writing and a pipeline that still needed him to check things such as thumbnails. After using it, he realised he didn't want to keep steering production. He still frequently wanted to edit the articles.

So he was reworking the pipeline with Lara, his agent, as its editor and orchestrator. She would move it through the stages and do the checks, asking him to look when something needed his attention. He could stay out of Snack Studio for routine production, then open it when he wanted to work on the writing himself.

Pete recognised the appeal. He said he no longer used Wingman directly for most of his work, though the agent software was still running on his home computer. He wouldn't give it up. When something went wrong, or he wanted more information, he could open it and see the details of what was happening.
