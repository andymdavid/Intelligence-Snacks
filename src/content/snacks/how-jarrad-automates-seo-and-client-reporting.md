Jarrad takes SEO seriously in his web builds. He's seen launches go badly, so he built a system using the self-hosted tool Open SEO and a repository that spins up for each new site. Getting it running took him two to three days.

The repository gathers research and audits, the existing sitemap and metadata, and information on which pages perform well. Jarrad says running reports at the start of a project gives him useful information quickly. Now he maintains the system at a simple level.

He also uses Grok Bot for recurring client work, setting up groups for each client with bots for functions such as engineering, security and SEO. Every Monday, reports are generated as artefacts and sent to his own product for review. He says his security bot looks for newly announced vulnerabilities and patches his infrastructure.
