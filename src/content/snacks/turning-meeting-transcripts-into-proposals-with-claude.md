Dr Mitch went shopping for replacement boots with his AirPods in. As he browsed, the Unveil business consultant dictated instructions into Claude, asking it to use yesterday’s meeting transcripts to prepare work for him to validate when he got back. He was gone for an hour or an hour and a half. On his return, multiple parts of the project were ready to read through and approve.

He’d already done the setup. Claude drew on Unveil’s resources to prepare proposals and service agreements, using templates and roadmaps suited to the client’s stage. Mitch had built the skills, processes and supporting systems beforehand. While shopping, he could call on that work by voice rather than assemble each document himself.

Mitch reckoned that, when he joined Unveil nearly four years earlier, the work from that shopping trip would have taken at least a week. He’d have been opening Canva and Excel, retrieving budget templates and pulling an NDA from SharePoint, and checking the service agreement’s version and branding. That paperwork still had to happen. But his promise to clients was to help their businesses grow and increase in value. Preparing the documents while he was elsewhere left him to read through and approve them.
