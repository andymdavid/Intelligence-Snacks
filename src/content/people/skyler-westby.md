Skyler Westby is a designer and entrepreneur associated with Finite Supply and [Finite Computer](https://finite.computer/). Finite Computer makes AI accessible to people who do not develop software, combining in-person training with simple software tools.

His work on Intelligence Snacks connects product design with the changing role of software. He explores agents that create tools around a person's immediate needs, ownership of the agents working inside a business, and the value of human judgement as generating software becomes easier. His interest in technology is tied to a practical aim: helping people spend more time with other people and less time in front of screens.
