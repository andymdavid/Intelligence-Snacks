Skyler Westby (often known online as Skyler Finite) is a digital product designer and entrepreneur. He is best known as the founder of Finite Supply, a Bitcoin-focused design agency, and finite.computer, a tech initiative aimed at simplifying technology and shifting away from modern hyper-connected screens.
