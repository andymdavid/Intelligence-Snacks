Kane Leersen is an Australian financial professional, community leader, and former athlete based in Colac, Victoria. He balances a prominent career in regional wealth management with deep roots in local country football and philanthropy. 

Raised as a fifth-generation sheep shearer's son, he successfully transitioned from his rural roots into wealth management, earning both a Commerce degree and an MBA. Today, he serves as the Organisation Leader and Personal Financial Adviser at Regional Prosperity, a firm dedicated to delivering transparent, values-driven financial advice to regional families and businesses. 

With over a decade of industry experience, he champions the belief that prosperity should not be limited by geographic boundaries. His community-first leadership approach has earned widespread industry recognition, including being named to The Inside Network's "40 Under 40" list and receiving a nomination for Thought Leader of the Year.
