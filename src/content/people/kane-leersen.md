Kane Leersen is Organisation Leader and Personal Financial Adviser at [Regional Prosperity](https://regionalprosperity.au/our-prosperity/), a financial advice firm serving regional Victoria. Based in Colac, he combines financial advice with responsibility for the people and systems that support the business.

In conversations about building the firm, Kane describes himself as a custodian with a longer horizon than his own tenure. He has spoken about hiring for character, developing people and using technology to support the team. His regional perspective is grounded in serving families and businesses where professional relationships extend into the wider community, and where continuity depends on passing on both knowledge and responsibility.

Kane's background spans a family shearing tradition, a commerce degree and an MBA. His work emphasises relationships, community and building a firm that can serve future generations. On Intelligence Snacks, he discusses the practical opportunities AI offers small businesses and the importance of preserving knowledge and capability through changes in ownership.
