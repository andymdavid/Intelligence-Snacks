Shawn Yeager is the founder of [Upshift](https://upshiftco.com/), where he helps professional services firms turn AI into services customers will pay for. His work connects decisions about products, pricing and sales with the systems needed to deliver them.

Shawn has spent three decades working on the commercial side of emerging technology. He started on Microsoft's first browser team and went on to work in hosting infrastructure at Exodus, connected hardware, personal data ownership and Bitcoin payments. His experience includes Bottlepay, NYDIG and Amboss, alongside advising founders working with new technologies.

On Intelligence Snacks, Shawn discusses what professional services firms can sell when AI reduces the hours needed to produce their work. He explores the value of trust and judgement, how firms can package expertise into repeatable services, and why client relationships still need human attention. The conversation also considers how junior professionals can gain experience as firms automate routine assignments.

He writes about technology and its commercial consequences on [his website](https://shawnyeager.com/) and through [Sideband](https://sideband.pub/).
