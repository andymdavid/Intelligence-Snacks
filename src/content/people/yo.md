Yo is a builder involved in [Sovereign Engineering](https://sovereignengineering.io/), a programme bringing developers together in Madeira to build tools around Bitcoin, Lightning, Nostr and related open technologies. Sovereign Engineering's public reports credit Yo as a cohort captain and as the builder of CSH, a web-terminal project using Nostr and ContextVM.

On Intelligence Snacks, Yo discusses how AI has changed freedom-tech development and what still requires a programmer's attention. His perspective combines rapid experimentation with the need to understand, review and maintain the code being produced. He also appears in Sovereign Engineering's No Solutions conversations about permissionless technology.
