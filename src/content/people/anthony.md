Anthony, known online as DeadmanOz, is an independent Bitcoin researcher and open-source builder based in Perth. His [public research site](https://deadmanoz.xyz/) covers Bitcoin's peer-to-peer network, merge mining, consensus failures and quantum-computing risks. His professional profile lists a PhD in systems engineering.

His writing moves between explaining how Bitcoin works and publishing investigations that other researchers can examine. Recent examples include a catalogue of invalid Bitcoin blocks, a walkthrough of how auxiliary chains reuse Bitcoin's proof-of-work, and analysis of the network's different node roles. That combination of software, measurement and explanation provides the background to his interest in AI as a research tool, rather than a substitute for checking the evidence.

His projects include Merge Mining Monitor and research into post-quantum Bitcoin. He also documents using AI agents to investigate Bitcoin networking with telemetry, analytical tools and source code. On Intelligence Snacks, he brings a researcher's perspective to local models, private agents and the limits of automated analysis.
