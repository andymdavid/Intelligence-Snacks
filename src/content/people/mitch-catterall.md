Dr. Mitch Catterall is Managing Partner at [Unveil Development Partners](https://unveildp.com/), working with Australian business owners on strategy, leadership and the systems needed to reduce dependence on the founder. Based in Wollongong, he works across professional services and skilled trades, helping owners turn knowledge held in their heads into processes their teams can use.

Mitch brings a background in education, business mentorship and blue-collar work. He holds a PhD from the University of Melbourne and moved from an academic career into business advisory. His approach combines practical business diagnostics with leadership development and implementation alongside owners and their teams.

His experience in his family's fencing business informs that work. In his [Unlocking Business interview](https://www.hypotential.com/podcast/unveil-development-partners-mitch-catterall/), he describes how the business struggled when his father could no longer work, shaping his focus on building companies with value beyond the owner's personal involvement. On Intelligence Snacks, he discusses practical AI adoption in small businesses, from client proposals and follow-up to custom software, while retaining human judgement and relationships.
