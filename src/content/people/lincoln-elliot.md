Lincoln Elliot is the founder of [Lexcen Consulting](https://lexcenconsulting.com.au/), a Perth-based independent consultancy working on strategy, performance and digital solutions. He also founded [Leen](https://leen.consulting/), a platform intended to help independent consultants find one another, form teams and develop business together.

His consulting career includes work at Accenture, as well as strategy and marketing work at Churchill Consulting and a role as Head of Consumer Marketing at Allianz. He chairs Catch Music, an organisation that brings people together through music. In the episode, he describes his experience leaving large consulting structures and the challenge of finding the right collaborators for clients while remaining independent.

Lincoln argues that a trusted network can give independent consultants access to colleagues, complementary skills and opportunities without recreating the overheads of a large firm. His work on Leen grew from that experience of building a team around an independent practice.
