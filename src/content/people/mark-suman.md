Mark Suman is co-founder and CEO of [Maple](https://maple.ai/), a personal AI platform built around private conversations. His work focuses on making privacy usable for everyday people, with the familiar experience they expect from an AI assistant.

Before Maple, Mark spent six years at Apple working on AI and machine learning with a focus on privacy. Earlier, he helped build Instructure Canvas and led its mobile work. He brings more than twenty years of experience in consumer technology, product and engineering to Maple.

In Episode 57, Mark describes why a private AI product still has to compete on user experience. He discusses the things people hesitate to share with an AI service, the trade-offs of local and cloud models, and the need to control what agents can send outside a private environment.
