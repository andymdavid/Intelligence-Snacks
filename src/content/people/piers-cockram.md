Piers Cockram is the founder and director of mineracks, an Australian company based in Brisbane that focuses on Bitcoin infrastructure, self-custody tools, and sovereign technology like self-hosted cloud storage and private AI. 

Through his current venture, mineracks, Piers has expanded his work past traditional Bitcoin mining into what he calls "the next generation of sovereign technology". 

This philosophy focuses on data ownership, open-source tech, and decentralized infrastructure; including Creating self-hosted cloud storage setups and private AI solutions so individuals and businesses do not have to rely on centralized Big Tech servers, and setting up localized, hosted mining facilities in Brisbane while designing hands-on simulations to make the learning curve for Bitcoin self-custody easier.
